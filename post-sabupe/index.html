End-to-End Business Intelligence & Analytics Project

Sales & Business Performance

Reproducible B2B construction materials analytics solution (SEED=42, 2024–2026) across 8 Lima zones, loading stg_demo1 and a Star Schema (dw_demo1) in SQL Server with pytest checks and DAX measures for Power BI.

150k Orders

Synthetic 2024–2026 B2B data (40k / 48k / 62k yearly) across 2,000 customers, 500 products & 50 salespeople.

SQL Server DW

stg_demo1 staging & dw_demo1 star schema (FactSales + 6 dimensions) with persisted financial columns.

4 Phases

Orchestrated via main.py (--phase 1..4 / --all) with automated pytest validation suites.

DAX & Power BI

Cataloged measures in dax/measures.md (margin, YoY/YTD, Pareto) reconciled against SQL aggregates.

Pipeline & Analytics Flow

1. Generate

Deterministic Python scripts (SEED=42) outputting CSVs to data/generated.

2. Staging

DDL & batch CSV ingestion into stg_demo1 (Completed, Cancelled, Pending).

3. Star Schema

Loads dw_demo1 dimensions and FactSales for Completed orders only.

4. SQL & PyTest

Reconciles counts, revenue, cost & gross profit between staging and FactSales.

5. DAX & BI

Semantic model & Power BI dashboards covering trends, customers, products & regions.

Core Business Rules & Analytical Patterns

Completed Orders & Attribution

FactSales filters to Completed orders and derives commercial RegionKey from the assigned salesperson's region.

Seasonality & 2025 Cost Shock

Models Jun–Oct peak season (9% monthly weight) and a 1.14x Steel & Rebar cost spike in Aug–Oct 2025 with price lag from Sep 2025.

Consistent KPI & DAX Definitions

Computes Revenue, TotalCost, and GrossProfit in SQL and aggregates margins, YoY/YTD, and Pareto ranks in DAX.
